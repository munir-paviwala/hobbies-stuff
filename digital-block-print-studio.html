Digital Block Print Studio

My attempt at bringing Indian block printing to the browser — the kind where a woodcarver stamps the same motif across cloth again and again, creating something bigger than the sum of its parts.

We've loaded a starter leaf motif to show you the ropes. Hit Print to Fabric to tile it, then draw over it and make it yours.

1. The left grid is your stamp — draw your motif there
2. Pick a pattern mode above the big canvas
3. Hit 🪷 Print to Fabric to tile it across the cloth
